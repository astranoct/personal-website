Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026 (reminders)

The short version: your calendars stay on your phone and with the calendar services you connect. PolyCal has no servers of its own and its developer never receives your calendar data. Anonymous usage statistics are sent only if you turn them on.

This policy explains how the PolyCal app ("PolyCal", "the app") handles information. PolyCal is developed by Victoria Wren ("I", "me"). For any question about this policy, email support@victoriawren.dev.

Information stored on your device

PolyCal stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Calendars and events from Google Calendar, from .ics files you import, from calendar links you subscribe to, and events you create in PolyCal.
  • Your settings, such as theme, language, calendar colors and reminder preferences.
  • Scheduled reminders, if you turn them on.

This data is not sent to me or to anyone other than the calendar services described below.

Google Calendar

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you do, PolyCal uses Google Sign-In and asks for permission to:

  • View your calendars and events (calendar.readonly), to display them in the app.
  • Create, edit and delete events (calendar.events), only when you choose to save, change or delete an event in a Google calendar. This is requested the first time you do so.

PolyCal also receives your Google account's email address, which is shown in the app so you know which account is connected.

Calendar data travels directly between your phone and Google over an encrypted connection. It is stored on your phone, and is never transferred to me, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. I cannot see your calendars.

PolyCal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google at any time in PolyCal (Calendars → Disconnect, or Settings → Delete all data), or from your Google Account permissions. Disconnecting removes PolyCal's access; your Google Calendar itself is not changed.

Calendar files and subscription links

When you import a .ics file, PolyCal reads it on your phone. When you subscribe to a calendar link, PolyCal downloads it directly from the address you entered. The operator of that address can see the request, as with any download, and their own privacy policy applies.

Reminders and background sync (optional)

If you turn on event reminders (Settings → Reminders), PolyCal asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules reminders on your phone. They are created and shown entirely on your device; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

While reminders are on, PolyCal also refreshes your calendars in the background about every six hours, so reminders stay up to date. This contacts Google Calendar (if connected) and the calendar links you subscribed to, exactly as a manual sync does. Turning reminders off stops it.

You can hide event details from reminders shown on your lock screen in Settings → Reminders.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

PolyCal can send anonymous usage statistics to help me understand which features are used. This is off unless you turn it on in Settings → Privacy, and you can turn it off at any time.

If you turn it on, PolyCal uses Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service, with data stored in the European Union. It sends:

WhatExamples
Which features are usedApp opened, screen viewed (Upcoming, Week, Month, Calendars), event saved or deleted (and whether it was in a local or Google calendar), calendar imported or subscribed, Google connected, theme or language changed
Basic technical detailsOperating system and version, app version, device language, whether it's a test build

It never includes your calendar content: no event titles, times, places, descriptions, attendees, calendar names or email addresses. There is no advertising ID, no device or user identifier and no cookies. Each app session gets a temporary random identifier that is not stored on your phone. Statistics are not linked to you and are only viewed in aggregate.

Turning statistics off, or using Delete all data, discards any statistics waiting on your phone to be sent.

Emails to support

If you email me, I receive whatever you include, plus the app and Android version that the "Contact support" button adds to the message. I use it only to answer you, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

What I don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking libraries.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No accounts: you never sign up for PolyCal itself.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Settings → Delete all data removes every calendar, event and setting stored by PolyCal, disconnects Google and revokes PolyCal's access. Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data. Because I don't receive your calendar data and statistics aren't linked to you, there is nothing held about you elsewhere to delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or PIPEDA in Canada) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Usage statistics are only collected with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time. For any request, email support@victoriawren.dev.

Children

PolyCal is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections to Google, to calendar links (when they use https) and to Aptabase are encrypted. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
