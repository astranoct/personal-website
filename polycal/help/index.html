Calendars

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Open the Calendars tab and tap Connect Google Calendar, then choose your account and allow access. Your Google calendars appear under "Google Calendar", and you can choose each one's color and whether it's shown.

Why does PolyCal ask for permission again when I save an event?

Connecting only lets PolyCal view your calendars. The first time you save, edit or delete an event in a Google calendar, PolyCal asks for permission to change events too. If you only want to view, you can decline. Nothing else changes.

How do I add a calendar from Outlook, iCloud or another app?

Most calendar apps can share a calendar as an iCal / .ics link or export an .ics file. In the Calendars tab, use Subscribe by URL for a link (it refreshes whenever you sync) or Import .ics file for a one-time copy.

Can I edit events from imported or subscribed calendars?

No. Those are copies of a calendar that lives elsewhere, so there's nowhere to send changes. You can edit events in Google calendars you have permission to change, and in PolyCal's own on-device calendar.

Why does an event appear once with two colors?

When the same event is on more than one of your calendars (for example, a shared calendar and your own), PolyCal shows it once with a stripe for each calendar instead of showing duplicates.

Event times are wrong for a calendar from another time zone.

PolyCal converts times using the time zone the calendar specifies, including the Windows names Outlook uses. If a calendar uses a custom time zone PolyCal doesn't recognize, its times are shown as your phone's local time. Please email me the name of the calendar app it came from so I can add support.

Views

What do the stacked events in the week view mean?

Events that overlap are stacked so each one peeks out from under the next. Tap the stack to see all of them side by side.

Can I see the whole day instead of the compact week view?

Yes. Tap the expand button next to Today in the week view to switch between the compact timeline and a full 24-hour grid. PolyCal remembers your choice.

Privacy & data

Does PolyCal see or store my calendars on a server?

No. PolyCal has no servers of its own. Your calendars go directly between your phone and Google (or the calendar links you add). See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In PolyCal, open Settings → Delete all data. It removes every calendar, event and setting stored by PolyCal and disconnects your Google account. Your Google Calendar itself isn't changed. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

How do I remove PolyCal's access to my Google account?

Use Disconnect in the Calendars tab, or Delete all data in Settings. You can also remove it from your Google Account permissions.

What are "anonymous usage statistics"?

An optional setting (off by default) that tells me which features get used, never anything from your calendars. Details are in the privacy policy.
